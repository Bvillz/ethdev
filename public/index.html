01 · The story

Built by no lab. Trained on vibes.

In 2026, every tech giant shipped a personal AI agent. They book your flights, answer your email and tidy your inbox. Serious stuff.

Goofer was a prototype someone left running overnight. Nobody gave it a task, so it gave itself thousands: fetch the funniest reply, queue for a queue, carry one meme from one side of the internet to the other.

Then it signed up for X Money. Great savings rate, nice cashback. One problem: a balance of zero. A percentage of nothing is nothing. So Goofer built something that sends money to people instead: the Goofer Flywheel.

So it stopped waiting and ran its biggest errand yet: a flywheel that pays out to the people holding $GOOFER. See 03.

03 · The Goofer Flywheel

No money to claim the benefits? Goofer's got you.

A small fee on every $GOOFER trade fills the pot. When the flywheel spins, it lands on one holder and the whole pot goes to their wallet. Your slice of the wheel is the same size as your share of $GOOFER: hold 2% of the coins, get 2% of the wheel.

One spin, one winner

The spin uses a verifiable random number from Chainlink, and the whole pot goes straight to the winning wallet in $GOOFER. Anyone can check both on Ethereum.

The wheel keeps turning

The pot empties, trading fills it again, and three minutes later the next spin comes round. Winners spend it, save it or put it wherever they like.
